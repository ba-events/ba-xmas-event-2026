🏠 Home Decor Contest

🎄 JINGLE BELLS 2026
HOME DECOR CONTEST 🎄

✨ Let your home sparkle with the magic of Christmas! Get creative, decorate your spaces and showcase your Christmas spirit!

🏡 How to Participate?

Round 1 – Video Submission

  • Record a video (maximum 2 minutes) showcasing your Christmas decorations.
  • Highlight your decorated entrance, balcony, windows, living spaces or other festive corners.
  • Keep the video simple, clear and focused on your decorations.
  • Submit your video before the announced deadline.
  • One entry per household.

🏆 Round 2 – Final Judging

  • The top 5 entries will be shortlisted.
  • Our judging panel will visit the 5 shortlisted homes.
  • The final winner will be selected based on the judges' evaluation during the home visits.

✨ What Will Be Judged?

  • Creativity & Originality
  • Christmas Theme & Festive Spirit
  • Overall Presentation
  • Lighting & Decoration
  • DIY & Sustainable Ideas
  • Attention to Detail

📌 A Few Things to Remember

  • Creativity matters more than the budget!
  • Keep decorations safe and avoid obstructing common areas.
  • Judges' decisions will be final.
